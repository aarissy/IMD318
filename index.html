3rd Week:

Lets Bake Chocolate Chip Cookies!!

  • Butter
  • Granulated Sugar
  • Eggs
  • Vanilla
  • Flour
  • Brown Sugar
  • Baking Soda
  • Salt
  • Chocolate Chip

Steps

    1. Mix butter and both sugar until smooth
    2. Adds eggs and vanilla extract
    3. Mix in flour and baking soda
    4. Fold in chocolate chips
    5. Shape the dough into small balls and place on a baking tray
    6. Bake at 180 C for 10-12 minutes
    7. Let the cookies cool before serving
